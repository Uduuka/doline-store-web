## 25. Messages `[Mixed]`

The store's own messaging: a team chat for everyone at the store, direct
messages between staff, and conversations with DolineMarket buyers. Reached from
**Inbox** in the menu drawer, which shows how many conversations are unread.

Messaging comes with Duka, Business and Enterprise. On Free the Inbox says
"Message your team -- Chat with your staff from any till, with Duka, Business or
Enterprise." and the owner gets **See plans**. Everyone can use the team chat
and their own direct messages; conversations with buyers are for the owner and
staff with the "Message clients" permission (section 21).

### Where messages are kept

Messages are written to the device first, like sales, and the device is where
they are kept. The cloud only carries them between devices: it keeps text for
30 days and photos, voice notes and videos for 7. A till that was off for longer
than that gets them from another device instead (Share messages, section 20).
To keep your own copy, use Google Drive backup (section 20). Signing a device out
deletes its messages (section 9).

### The Inbox

![The Inbox: All and Unread, then Test Cashier (Direct message), Test Buyer (DolineMarket buyer) and Team chat (Everyone at this store)](https://downloads.dolinesystems.com/manual/25-inbox.png)

Headed "Inbox". **All** and **Unread** at the top; "Unread · 2" counts what is
waiting. Each row is one conversation with its name, what kind it is -- "Team
chat · Everyone at this store", "Direct message", "DolineMarket buyer" -- the last
message and its time, and a count of unread messages. Tapping a row opens it. On
a tablet the conversation opens beside the list ("Pick a conversation -- It opens
here, beside the list.").

The team chat is always there once the till has been online. Empty, the Inbox
says "Say hello in the team chat, or start a message with anyone at this store."
and Unread with nothing new says "Nothing new since you last looked."

**New message** (the + at the top right) lists everyone else at the store to
start a direct message with. "Nobody else here yet. Add staff in Settings >
Staff." when the shop has only you.

![New message: the list of staff to message](https://downloads.dolinesystems.com/manual/25-new-message.png)

"Offline: messages you send will go when this till is back online." shows when
the till has no connection: you can still write, and messages go when it
reconnects. Before the till has first synced, the Inbox says "Not ready yet --
Messages open once this till has synced with the cloud."

### A conversation

![The team chat: messages, a photo, a video, people joining from their phones, a voice note, and the message box with photo, video and microphone buttons](https://downloads.dolinesystems.com/manual/25-team-chat.png)

Your messages are on the right, others' on the left with their name and role.
Times are under each message. A line in the middle such as "Test Cashier joined
from their phone" records a staff member adding their own phone (section 6).

The message box is at the bottom:

- **Type a message…** -- then the send button.
- **Send a photo** -- pick one; "Photo · add a caption below, or just send".
- **Send a video** -- pick one. A video longer than 2 minutes sends its first 2
  minutes ("Only the first 2 minutes are sent."), brought down to 720p first
  ("Preparing video…"); one still over 50 MB after that is refused ("This video is
  too big to send, even at 720p.").
- **Record a voice note** -- the microphone, shown while the box is empty. Tap
  it to start; see below.

A message that could not go shows why under it -- "Not sent yet: too many at
once", "Not sent: messaging isn't on this plan", "Not sent: this conversation is
closed" -- and tapping it sends it again.

### Voice notes

![Recording a voice note: the cancel button, Recording 0:02, max 5:00, and send](https://downloads.dolinesystems.com/manual/25-recording.png)

Tap **Record a voice note** and speak. The box shows "Recording 0:02" and "max
5:00": a note can be up to five minutes, and sends itself at five. The send
button sends it; **Cancel the voice note** throws it away. The first time,
Android asks to let Doline Store record audio. If that was refused: "Doline
Store needs the microphone for voice notes. Allow it in Android's settings."

A voice note plays in the conversation with its length ("Voice note · 0:04").

### Photos, voice notes and videos you receive

Photos and voice notes download by themselves. Videos download by themselves
on Wi-Fi; on mobile data a video waits as "Tap to download · 12.4 MB" so it
does not use your data without asking. A video shows its first picture and
length; tap it to play. Media the cloud no longer has reads "No longer available"; media
cleared from this device (section 20, Message storage) reads "Removed from this
device"; and media that is in a Google Drive backup reads "In your Google Drive
backup".

### Editing, deleting and reporting

![Holding down someone else's message: Report to Doline](https://downloads.dolinesystems.com/manual/25-message-menu.png)

Hold a message down for its menu:

- **Edit** -- your own text messages, for 15 minutes after sending. "Editing your
  message" shows while you do; the message then carries "edited" beside its
  time.
- **Delete for everyone** -- your own messages, for 24 hours ("It's removed for
  everyone in this conversation."). The owner can also delete any staff member's
  message in that time, but never a buyer's. It
  leaves "Message deleted" with when it was sent and deleted.
- **Report to Doline** -- anyone else's message: "What's wrong?" -- "Abusive, a
  scam, spam…". "Doline support sees it with the 10 messages before it, and who
  sent them. They don't tell anyone who reported."

### Conversations with buyers

![A conversation with a DolineMarket buyer](https://downloads.dolinesystems.com/manual/25-buyer-conversation.png)

A buyer's conversation starts from **Message the buyer** on their marketplace
order (section 15) or **Message this client** on a linked client (section 16),
and from the buyer's side in DolineMarket. An order's messages are cleared a day
after the order is settled.

The menu at the top of a buyer's conversation has **Block** ("Neither of you can
write in it until you unblock it. You can still read it.") and, once blocked,
**Unblock**. While blocked the box is replaced by "You blocked this
conversation. Nobody can write in it until you unblock it." -- or "The buyer
blocked this conversation." when it was them.

![A buyer conversation's menu: Block](https://downloads.dolinesystems.com/manual/25-conversation-menu.png)

Staff with "Message clients" read every buyer's conversation, but a direct
message between two people is theirs: anyone else sees "You can read this
conversation, but only its two members write in it."

### When Doline support steps in

If a report shows the store's messages being misused, Doline support can pause
them. The Inbox then says "Doline support paused this store's messages:
<reason>", and a conversation support closed says "Doline support closed this
conversation."

### On a staff member's own phone

A staff member who joined with an invite (section 6) has only Messages on their
phone: the same conversations they would see at a till, behind the phone's own
screen lock. The store's tills and the owner's devices make backups and share
messages; the staff phone does neither.
