## 3. Register `[Both]`

Creates the account, and with it the shop's owner and first admin. The screen is
headed "Create account" and opens from Get started on the Welcome screen.

![Create account: Full name, Email address, optional Phone number, the Terms checkbox, Continue, and Continue with Google](https://downloads.dolinesystems.com/manual/03-register.png)

Do this once per shop. Everyone else who needs Doline is added later as staff,
from Settings > Staff & Security, under this same account -- not by creating
accounts of their own. (A staff member who wants the store's messages on their
own phone does make a Doline account, but only to join the store with an
invite; see section 6.)

### The form

There is no password. Doline confirms who you are with a code sent to your
email instead, every time you sign in on a new device.

- **Full name** -- your own name, for example "Sarah Namuli". It is what appears
  on the staff lock, so staff pick the right person.
- **Email address** -- the account's address. The sign-in code is sent here, and
  so is the code that resets a forgotten admin pass key, so use an inbox you
  actually read rather than a shared address nobody checks. A mistyped address
  is caught straight away: "That doesn't look like an email address."
- **Phone number (optional)** -- a Uganda mobile number. +256 is already filled
  in; type the nine digits after it, starting with 7. Anything else is refused
  with "Enter the 9 digits after +256, starting with 7."
- **I agree to Doline's Terms and Privacy Policy** -- tick it. Continue stays
  unavailable until you do.

**Continue** sends a six-digit code to the address and moves to the code screen
(section 5). "Already have an account? Log in" at the foot goes to the Log in
screen instead, and the back arrow returns to Welcome.

### Continue with Google

Under the form, after "or", **Continue with Google** creates the account with a
Google account instead of an email code. It opens Google's own sign-in, and comes
back once you have picked an account. Closing Google's screen without picking one
comes back as "Google sign-in was cancelled." -- nothing has been created; try
again or use the form. Signing in with Apple is not available.

### If something goes wrong

- "There's no account with this email. Create one instead." appears on Log in,
  not here -- it is how Log in tells you to come back to this screen.
- "We can't create accounts just now. Try again shortly." -- new accounts are
  briefly not being accepted. Nothing was made.
- "Too many codes sent. Wait a minute, then try again." -- several codes were
  asked for in a short time.
- "No connection. Check your internet and try again." -- creating an account
  needs the internet, unlike selling.

### What happens next

After the code is accepted, a short "Setting up your account…" screen fetches
your profile and the standard product categories. Then you choose how you will
use Doline (section 6) -- for a new shop owner, "This is my business" -- and set
the admin pass key. The shop itself does not exist yet: you create it a step
later, from the store picker (section 7).
