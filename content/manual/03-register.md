## 3. Register `[Both]`

Creates the account, and with it the shop's first admin. Headed "Welcome, Create
your account", reached from Get Started on the welcome screen.

Do this once per shop. Every other person who needs to use Doline is added later
as staff, from Settings, under this same account -- not by registering again.

On a computer, it is "Create account" at the foot of the Log in screen: full
name, email address, an optional phone number and agreeing to the terms, then
the 6-digit code emailed to that address. "Continue with Google" is under it.
After the code, the computer goes on to create the admin pass key, then to the
stores list, where "New store" makes the shop's first store.

### Registering with Google

A single GOOGLE button sits at the top, above an "OR CONTINUE WITH" divider.
Tapping it leaves the app for Google's own sign-in page in a browser and comes
back once you have chosen an account. Signing in with Apple is not available.

If you close the browser without finishing, the app notices on the way back and
says "Google sign-in was cancelled." Nothing has been created; start again or
use the form below instead.

### The form

Five fields, in this order:

- **Email address** -- the account's address. It is what you sign in with on
  every other device, and where a password reset link would be sent, so use one
  you can actually read rather than a shared shop address nobody checks.
- **Username** -- a short name for the account.
- **Full names** -- your own name. This is what appears in the staff list on the
  lock screen, so staff pick the right person.
- **Password** -- at least six characters. The eye icon at the end of the field
  shows what you have typed.
- **Confirm password** -- the same password again.

The REGISTER button at the bottom submits. "Already have an account? Login"
below it goes to the Login screen.

### What it tells you when something is wrong

Problems appear in a red block above the Register button, and nothing is created
until they are cleared.

- "All fields are required." -- one of the five is empty. Confirm password is
  the one most often missed.
- "Passwords do not match." -- the two password fields differ. Use the eye icon
  to see what you actually typed.
- "Password must be at least 6 characters." -- six is the minimum the app
  accepts, here and when resetting a password later.
- "Registration failed. The email may already be in use." -- most often that
  address already has a Doline account, in which case Login, not Register, is
  what you want.

### Confirming your email

Depending on how the account is configured, registering may not sign you
straight in. Instead a message appears saying "We sent a confirmation link to
<your address>. Verify it, then log in." Open the link from your email, then
come back and use Login.

If it does not arrive, "Didn't get it? Resend email" sits directly under that
message. Check the spelling of the address first -- it is shown back to you in
the message precisely so a typo is obvious.

### What happens next

A successful registration goes straight on to a short setup screen, which
fetches your profile and the standard product categories, and then to the admin
pass key (section 6). Your shop itself does not exist yet -- you create it a step
later, from the store picker.
