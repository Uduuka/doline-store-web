## 4. Login `[Both]`

Signs in to an account that already exists. Headed "Welcome back," and reached
from Login on the welcome screen.

This is the screen for setting up a second till, and for a device that has been
signed out. Sign in as the shop's admin account -- a staff member's own name and
pass key belong on the lock screen later, not here. Doline has one account per
shop, and staff live inside it.

### What is on it

- **GOOGLE** -- sign in with the Google account the shop registered with, above
  an "OR CONTINUE WITH" divider. Signing in with Apple is not available.
- **Email address** -- the address the account was registered with.
- **Password** -- its password, with an eye icon to show what you typed.
- **Forgot password?** -- directly under the password field. Sends a reset link
  by email; see section 5.
- **Login** -- the blue button that submits, with an arrow on its right.
- **Register** -- at the foot: "Don't have an account? Register", for a shop
  that is not on Doline yet.

Below everything sits a copyright line, "© 2026 DOLINE POS. ALL RIGHTS
RESERVED." It is not a control.

### If it will not let you in

Failures appear in a red block above the Login button. A wrong password and an
unknown address both read much the same way, so check the address for typos
before assuming the password is wrong -- and if the account has never confirmed
its email, confirm that first from the link that was sent at registration.

Signing in needs a connection. On a device that is *already* set up this does
not matter, because the app does not ask you to sign in again -- it opens on the
staff lock, which works entirely offline. Being sent to this screen with no
network on a till that was working is a fault, not something to work around.

A Google sign-in abandoned halfway -- the browser closed without choosing an
account -- comes back as "Google sign-in was cancelled." Nothing has changed; try
again.

On a computer, "Continue with Google" opens the browser and the button reads
"Finish in your browser…" until Google answers. Once you have chosen an account,
the browser tab can be closed and the Doline window moves on by itself. The computer cannot tell
that a browser tab was closed, so press "Cancel" under the button to stop
waiting and try again.

### What happens next

A successful sign-in goes to a short setup screen that pulls down your profile,
your admin record and the standard product categories. Your shops are
deliberately *not* pulled here -- that waits for the store picker, so this device
only ever downloads the shops you actually choose for it. From there it is the
admin pass key (section 6).
