## 4. Login `[Both]`

Signs in to an account that already exists. Headed "Log in", with the line
"Welcome back. Enter your email and we'll send you a code to log in.", and
reached from Log in on the Welcome screen.

![Log in: the Email address field, Send code, Continue with Google, and Create account at the foot](https://downloads.dolinesystems.com/manual/04-login.png)

This is the screen for setting up a second till, for a device that has been
signed out, and for a staff member setting up their own phone for messages. On a
till, sign in as the shop's owner account -- a staff member's own name and pass
key belong on the staff lock later, not here.

### What is on it

- **Email address** -- the address the account was created with.
- **Send code** -- emails a six-digit code to that address and moves to the code
  screen (section 5). There is no password to remember.
- **Continue with Google** -- for an account that was created with Google.
- **New to Doline? Create account** -- at the foot, for a shop that is not on
  Doline yet (section 3).

A copyright line closes the screen and is not a control.

### If it will not let you in

Problems appear under the email field:

- "There's no account with this email. Create one instead." -- check the address
  for a typo first; if it is right, the account does not exist yet.
- "That doesn't look like an email address." -- something is missing, usually
  the part after the @.
- "Too many codes sent. Wait a minute, then try again." -- several codes were
  asked for in a short time.
- "No connection. Check your internet and try again."
- "Google sign-in was cancelled." -- Google's screen was closed without picking
  an account. Nothing changed.

Signing in needs a connection. On a device that is *already* set up this does
not matter, because the app does not ask you to sign in again -- it opens on the
staff lock, which works entirely offline. A till whose sign-in has lapsed keeps
selling for a day and shows a banner asking for the owner to sign it in again
(section 24); it is never a reason to wipe the device.

On a computer, "Continue with Google" opens the browser and the button reads
"Finish in your browser…" until Google answers. Once you have chosen an account,
the browser tab can be closed and the Doline window moves on by itself. Press
"Cancel" under the button to stop waiting and try again.

### What happens next

After the code, a short "Setting up your account…" screen pulls down your
profile and the standard product categories. Your shops are deliberately *not*
pulled yet -- that waits for the store picker, so this device only downloads the
shops you choose for it. Then comes "How will you use Doline Store?" and the
admin pass key (section 6).
