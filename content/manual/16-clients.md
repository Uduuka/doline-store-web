## 16. Clients `[Both]`

The shop's customers, reached from Clients in the menu drawer. A client is worth
creating for anyone you will sell to twice, and essential for anyone you sell to
on credit.

### The list

Headed "Clients", one row per client showing their name and their phone number
-- or their address if there is no phone, or "No contact details" if neither. A
client who owes the shop money also shows "Owes" and the amount on the right. The
search icon opens "Search clients"; the blue + button adds one. On a phone, pulling the list down syncs it (see the overview).

Empty, it reads "No clients added yet." or "No clients match ...".

### Adding a client

The + button opens a sheet headed "Add new client" with three fields -- Name,
Phone and Address -- and a Save button. Only the name is really needed, but the
phone is what makes the client useful later: it is what you call when a credit
balance has been sitting too long.

You can also add a client mid-sale from the cart, and it is saved to the shop
the same way (section 11).

### A client's own screen

Headed "Client", with their name and "Client since ..." underneath, then three
figures: **Orders** (how many), **Spent** (what they have paid for in total) and
**Owes** (what is still outstanding on their credit orders, with how many orders
that is). Then:

- **Phone** -- with a call button beside it that hands the number to the phone's
  own dialler, so you can ring a customer without copying the number out. "Not
  provided" if there is none.
- **Address** -- or "Not provided".
- **ORDERS** -- every order this client has placed: number, progress, amount and
  date. Tapping one opens it. "No orders placed yet." when there are none.

The menu in the top bar holds "Edit client", which reopens the same three
fields, and "Delete client" in red, which confirms by name and warns that it
cannot be undone.

### What a client owes

The Owes figure on the list and on the client's screen is the total still unpaid
across their credit orders, in the shop's currency. A payment you take against
credit (section 15) brings it down straight away on this device, even before it
has synced.

It is a running total, not a statement: there is no printable customer statement
or account history yet. To see which orders make up the balance, open the client
and read their ORDERS, or go to Orders and tap the Credit chip.
