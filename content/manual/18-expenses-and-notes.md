## 18. Expenses and Notes `[Both]`

Two small screens from the menu drawer. Expenses records what the shop spends;
Notes keeps writing with the shop rather than on somebody's phone.

### Expenses

![Expenses: today's date, Spent, Daily budget and Remaining](https://downloads.dolinesystems.com/manual/18-expenses.png)

Headed "Expenses", with a calendar icon in the top bar to pick a date and a +
button to add one. Each entry shows its category, its amount and its
description. Empty, it reads "No expenses recorded yet". On a phone, pulling the list down syncs it (see the overview).

At the top, "Total Expenses Today" adds up the day's spending. That figure is
real.

### The daily budget

Beside the day's total sit Daily Budget and Remaining. Tap the budget figure to
set it -- it reads "Tap to set" until you do -- and Remaining then counts down
from it as the day's expenses are recorded. Overspend and Remaining goes
negative and turns red, which is the point of the card.

The dialog also offers Clear, because a budget is a choice a shop can unmake.
With none set, Remaining says "No budget set" rather than reporting an overspend
against a number nobody chose.

The budget is kept on this device only. It is not synced, so a second till in
the same shop has its own, and setting it on the counter phone does not set it
on the stockroom tablet.

### Recording an expense

The + button opens "Add new Expense":

- **EXPENSE CATEGORY** -- one of six: Inventory, Utilities, Logistics, Staff,
  Marketing or Others. The list is fixed; you cannot add your own.
- **AMOUNT** -- what was spent, in the shop's currency.
- **DESCRIPTION** -- what it was for. Worth filling in; the category alone will
  not remind you in three months.

"Save Expense" records it against today.

Expenses are a record, not an accounting system: they do not come off the
revenue or profit figures in Reports, which are built from sales and stock cost
alone. Treat the two as separate books.

### Notes

![Notes: each note with its title, first line and when it was written](https://downloads.dolinesystems.com/manual/18-notes.png)

Headed "Notes", a list of cards each showing a note's title, its body and when
it was written, with a search field -- "Search for notes..." -- above. Empty, it
says "There are no notes." The + button writes a new one. On a phone, pulling
the notes down syncs them.

"Add new note" is deliberately plain: a title line ("Notes title") and a body
("Start writing your note here..."), then "Save Notes". The button stays
disabled until both have something in them.

Notes belong to the shop, not to you, so they sync and every till in the shop
sees them. That makes them the right place for a handover message, a supplier's
quoted price, or an instruction for whoever opens tomorrow -- and the wrong place
for anything private.
