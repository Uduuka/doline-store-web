## 23. Help and feedback `[Both]`

Headed "Help & Feedback", from the menu drawer. Four things on one screen: a
search, the FAQs, two support shortcuts, and a feedback form.

### What is on it

- **Search help articles** -- opens a search screen: "Search FAQs or help
  articles", with "No FAQs or articles matched your search." when nothing hits.
- **Frequently Asked Questions** -- browsable categories, each opening as "FAQ
  on <category>". See the warning below.
- **Call Us** -- under Quick support: the support number, +256300790380, handed
  to the phone's dialler.
- **Ask Lilli** -- also under Quick support, marked "AI Assistant": straight to
  section 22.
- **Submit Feedback** -- under "Help us improve": "Suggest a feature or report a
  bug."

### Do not trust the FAQ section

Its categories and answers are placeholder content left over from an earlier
draft of the product, and much of it describes features Doline does not have:
bulk inventory import from CSV, per-item low stock alert thresholds you can set
under an item's Edit screen, push notifications when stock drops, IoT sensors
and shelf cameras, warehouse management integration, invoices, predictive
analytics and CSV export. (Doline does now have plans -- section 24 -- but not as
the FAQ describes them.)

None of that exists. Following those instructions means hunting for screens that
are not there and concluding you are using the app wrong, when the FAQ is simply
wrong. The categories about product pricing and deliveries are closer to
reality, but the whole section should be read as unverified.

For a real answer, use this manual or ask Lilli, who answers navigation
questions from it rather than from the FAQs.

### Keeping Doline Store up to date

When a newer version is out, the app says so as it opens (and checks again every
few hours):

- **"Doline Store 1.6.4 is available"** -- with what is new, **Update** and
  **Later**. Later puts it off until the next version.
- **"Update Doline Store to continue"** -- this version is too old to sync with
  the cloud any more, so nothing else opens until it is updated. Anything not yet
  synced is kept on the phone and goes up once you have updated.

**Download update** fetches the new version in the browser; open the downloaded
file and tap **Update**. Install it over the version you have -- **don't
uninstall first**, because uninstalling deletes anything on the phone that has
not reached the cloud. If the phone got Doline Store from Google Play, the
button says **Update on Google Play** instead.

On versions 1.6.1 and older, the button always opens Google Play, where Doline
Store is not listed. Update those phones from the download page instead:
dolinesystems.com/products/doline-store/download.

### Sending feedback

The form asks "What's on your mind?" with four categories to choose from -- Bug
Report, Feature Suggestion, General Feedback and Other -- then Details
("Describe your feedback"), an "Allow follow-up via email" switch, and a "Send
Feedback" button.

The follow-up switch uses the email address on your account; it reads "No email
on file" if there is none, in which case nobody can reply to you.

- "Pick a category and add some details before submitting." -- both are
  required.
- "You're offline - reconnect and try again." -- feedback is sent immediately
  rather than queued, so it needs a connection, unlike a sale.
- "Couldn't send feedback. Please try again." -- it reached the network and
  failed. Try once more.
- "Thanks for your feedback!" -- it went through.

This is the right channel for the unfinished parts this manual has named -- the
Filters button on the till, the placeholder supplier and
budget figures, the settings that save but do nothing, the FAQ content. A bug
report naming the screen and what you expected is worth more than a general
complaint, and the manual exists partly so you can describe the gap precisely.
