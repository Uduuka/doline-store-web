## 1. Overview `[Both]`

Doline Store is the app you run your shop from. It is the till that takes
payment, the stock book that knows what is on the shelf, the order history, the
customer and supplier list, and the reports that tell you how the week went --
in one app, on an Android device behind the counter.

### Two things that shape everything else

**It keeps selling without internet.** Every sale, every stock change and every
new customer is written to the device first and sent up to the cloud afterwards,
whenever there is a connection again. You can open the app, ring up a sale and
close the till on a day the network never comes back. Being asked to sign in
again while offline is not normal behaviour -- it is a fault worth reporting.

**A device holds only the shops you install on it.** An account can own several
shops, and when a device is set up you choose which of them belong on it --
usually just the one it sits in. Nothing else about the account comes down. This
is why setting up a device asks you to pick, and why signing a device out clears
only that device: the data stays in the cloud and comes back the next time
someone signs in.

### Setting up a device, at a glance

A new install walks through this once. Afterwards the app opens on step five
every time, and you never see the earlier screens again unless the device is
signed out or reinstalled. Each step has its own section later in this manual.

1. **Welcome** -- the first screen on a device that has not been set up. Get
   Started creates a new account; Login signs in to one that exists.
2. **Register or sign in** -- with an email address and password, or a Google
   account. Whoever registers becomes the shop's admin.
3. **The admin pass key** -- a six digit key. The first device sets it; every
   later device asks for it before it will go any further.
4. **Choose the shops for this device** -- pick from the account's shops, or
   create the first one. Their settings, including the currency they price in,
   come down with them.
5. **Who's on shift?** -- from here on the app opens here: the staff lock.

### The staff lock

The counter device is shared, so Doline wants to know who is holding it before
it lets anyone sell anything. You tap your own name from the staff list and
enter your own six digit pass key. Everything you then do is recorded against
your name, which is what makes the staff figures in Reports mean anything.

The device re-locks itself when it has been left alone for a moment, so a device
put down on the counter does not stay open on the last person's shift. Wrong
pass keys cost you a wait that grows with repetition, and a forgotten pass key
is an admin reset. Section 8 has the details.

### Finding your way around

Past the lock, the app is five tabs along the bottom. These never move, and they
are always one tap away from wherever you are.

- **Store** -- the dashboard: the day at a glance, and the way in to everything
  that is not selling or stock.
- **Inventory** -- everything the shop stocks, item by item, with its batches
  and stock levels.
- **POS** -- the till. Search or scan an item, build a cart, take payment.
- **Orders** -- every sale that has been rung up, plus drafts and outstanding
  credit.
- **Lilli** -- the in-app assistant. Ask it about your own figures, or how to do
  something in the app.

On the Store tab, the menu button opens a drawer with the rest of the app --
nine destinations, in this order:

- **Dashboard** -- back to the Store tab itself.
- **Reports** -- sales, profit, stock health and restocking.
- **Expenses** -- what the shop has spent, recorded against the day.
- **Notes** -- notes kept with the shop rather than on someone's phone.
- **Clients** -- customers, their details and their orders.
- **Suppliers** -- who you buy from.
- **Settings** -- how this shop is configured, and the account's plan, devices
  and backups. Open to admins and to staff allowed to change store settings; five
  of its screens are admin-only.
- **Help and feedback** -- this manual inside the app, the FAQs, and a form for
  telling us what is wrong.
- **Lilli** -- the same assistant as the bottom tab.

The account icon at the top right of the dashboard holds your notifications --
a red dot on it means some are unread -- and the three things that are about
you rather than about the shop: Edit profile, Lock -- which returns the device
to the staff lock -- and Change pass key, which asks for your current pass key
once and the new one twice.

### Admins and staff

An admin may do everything. Every other staff member may do what an admin has
ticked for them on their staff form (section 21), on top of what everyone may
do.

- **Everyone:** sell for cash, add clients, record expenses and notes, count
  stock, look things up in Inventory, Orders, Clients and Suppliers, ask Lilli,
  and change their own profile and pass key.
- **By permission:** selling on credit, returns and cancellations, editing
  orders, receiving stock, correcting batches, writing off stock, settling stock
  counts, adding and editing items, prices and promotions, marketplace listings,
  suppliers, Reports, seeing costs and profit, and Settings.
- **Admin only:** the Store Details, Staff & Security, Activity, Plan and Active
  devices screens under Settings -- so adding and editing staff, their
  permissions and admin access, publishing the store on DolineMarket and signing
  other devices out -- deleting items, adding a shop to the account and reaching
  the shop list at all.

When someone tries something they are not allowed to, the app asks for an
admin's pass key there and then, for that one action ("Admin approval needed";
section 21). Everything that needed a permission is recorded in the Activity
log, with who did it and which admin allowed it.

Each section of this manual carries a tag -- `[Both]`, `[Admin only]` or
`[Mixed]` -- so you can tell at a glance whether a flow is one you can carry out
yourself or one to take to an admin. `[Mixed]` usually means a permission
decides it.

A non-admin device is also pinned to the shop it is open on. Staff cannot reach
the account's other shops or the shop list, which is deliberate: the stockroom
tablet should not be a way into the branch across town.

### What the cloud does for you

The device does the work; the cloud keeps the copies and joins the tills
together. In practice it earns its place in four ways.

- **Nothing is lost with a device.** A broken or stolen till costs you the
  device, not the shop's records. Sign in on a replacement, pick the same shop,
  and its stock, orders, clients and settings come back down.
- **Several tills agree.** Two counters in the same shop see the same stock and
  the same orders, once both have had a connection.
- **Online orders arrive.** Orders placed through the Doline marketplace come
  down into the same Orders tab as the ones rung up at the counter.
- **Your shop front is published.** Once the store is on DolineMarket (section
  24), what the shop takes in payment, whether it delivers and the currencies it
  prices in are shown to buyers before they order.

None of it is anything to operate. There is no upload to wait for -- the app
queues what you do and sends it when it can: moments later on a paid plan, or
once a day at a time you choose on Free. Settings > Backup & sync shows anything
on the device that has not gone up yet, and Back up now sends it straight away. To bring a screen up to date now
rather than at the next pass, pull its list down on a phone (the dashboard,
Inventory, Orders, Clients, Suppliers, Expenses, Notes and Reports all sync
this way), or press "Sync now" on desktop.

### Plans

Every account is on a plan -- Free, Duka, Business or Enterprise -- which sets how
many items, staff, devices and stores it can have, whether it sells on
DolineMarket, how often it backs up and how many questions Lilli answers a month.
Settings > Plan shows yours and how much of it you are using. Section 24 has the
details, including what happens when a limit is reached.

### How to read the rest

From here the manual follows the app rather than tasks: one section per screen,
in roughly the order you meet them, and within each section every control on the
screen.

Two promises about the writing. **Nothing here describes a feature that does not
exist**; where a button is on the screen but does nothing yet, the manual says
so rather than leaving it out, because you can see it and deserve to know. And
where a flow depends on your access level, the tag at the top of the section
tells you before you read it.
